# HERLIYAH owner guide

The owner dashboard preview is available at `/admin`. It shows the intended phone-friendly structure for products, preorder capacity, orders and settings, but protected writes are deliberately disabled.

Before launch, the owner must provide the final logo, photography, product names/descriptions/prices, measurements, care instructions, capacity model, preparation rules, delivery zones/fees/transit estimates, return and exchange policy, contact details, admin email, authentication provider, Paystack account and verified email sender.

Operationally, capacity means production allocation, not garments on a shelf. Increasing or reopening capacity needs a reason and audit entry. A refund does not automatically restore capacity. Delays keep the original promise, record a revised estimate and notify the customer. Production and courier delivery remain separate progress dimensions.

The proposed rules—full upfront payment, a 20-minute reservation and shipping mixed baskets together when the slowest style is ready—are configurable assumptions awaiting approval.
